Payments between Yorkshire and the Humber's industries, month by month (prototype)

Business-to-business payments where both sides are registered in the region. Buyers sit higher, suppliers lower. Hidden months are held at the cell's average published month.

Money runs down the order, buyer to supplier Money runs up the order Link is widest at the paying end; its width is the amount. Each dot is 100 payments that month, so a wide link with few dots is a few large payments and a thin crowded one is many small ones. Dashed: this month is hidden and held at an average. Circle area: payments made and received inside the region. Ring: the dark arc at the top is the share of the sector's income that comes from the rest of the UK, the arc at the bottom the share of its spending that goes there. Halo: the same two flows as areas, on the circle's scale.
